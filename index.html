Research & Projects

EpiProfile-PLANTS

MATLAB suite that extends EpiProfile 2.0 to plant histone proteomics. It brings curated peptide catalogues for three plant and algal species, a Snakemake + Docker workflow and a dashboard to read the output.

epiprofile-plants workflow dashboard

K-CHOPORE

Eight-stage Snakemake + Docker workflow for Oxford Nanopore direct RNA-seq, orchestrating 15 open-source tools from a single configuration file: basecalling, isoform analysis, m6A detection and differential expression, applied to Arabidopsis.

kchopore-anac017-drs

  • Arabidopsis Rosette Ontogeny Atlas

    Histone PTMs across four stages of rosette development, with the quality control done before the statistics.

  • Public PRIDE reanalyses

    Public proteomics datasets reprocessed end to end with the same pipeline.

  • Cancer genomics & transcriptomics

    RNA-seq, differential expression and multi-omics approaches to oncology research at CNIO.
